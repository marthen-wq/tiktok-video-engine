Shorts Engine

A personal tool that turns short motivational scripts into vertical videos and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts after the owner approves each video.

  • Only the account owner uses this tool. It does not offer accounts or services to other people.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.
  • Source code: github.com/marthen-wq/tiktok-video-engine
