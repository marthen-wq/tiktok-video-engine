Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Shorts Engine is a personal publishing tool operated by its owner for the owner's own social media accounts.

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information and permission to upload videos to the owner's account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload).

How data is used

Access tokens are used only to upload videos that the owner has approved. They are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets or in a private spreadsheet that only the owner can access.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. No data from other users is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok or Google account settings; stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.
